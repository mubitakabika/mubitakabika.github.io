MY IMPACT

Creating value beyond my profession.

My work extends beyond the classroom and newsroom. Through education, youth development, media, community engagement and creative initiatives, I seek to develop people, create opportunities and amplify meaningful stories.

01

Education & Skills Development

Through teaching and skills development, I contribute to helping learners acquire practical knowledge, digital skills and professional confidence that can be applied beyond the classroom.

02

Youth Empowerment

Through Tivannah Innovations Youth Organization and other youth-focused initiatives, I support young people in exploring creativity, leadership, entrepreneurship, media and constructive community participation.

03

Community Engagement

I participate in community-focused activities that encourage awareness, organisation, participation, responsibility and positive social development.

04

Journalism & Public Awareness

Through journalism, interviews and media production, I help bring community issues, public concerns and important stories to wider audiences.

05

Social Awareness Through Film

Film and storytelling provide platforms for discussing social issues, encouraging dialogue and bringing attention to challenges affecting individuals and communities.

06

Creative & Digital Communication

Through media, visual communication, photography, video and digital content, I help ideas, organisations and initiatives communicate with audiences more effectively.

MY APPROACH

Skills should create impact.

I believe professional skills become more meaningful when they are used to solve problems, develop people, communicate important ideas and contribute to the communities around us.

Whether I am teaching a learner, reporting a community story, working with young people, supporting a community initiative or developing a creative production, my goal is to make the skills I have useful beyond myself.

“The real measure of a skill is not only what it can achieve for you, but what it can make possible for others.”
Mubita Siasabuzingo Kabika

Lecturer • Journalist • Creative
