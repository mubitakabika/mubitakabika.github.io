Founder • Youth Development • Creative Leadership

Youth Development & Creative Work
04
YOUTH ADVOCACY

Youth Development & Community Organisation

Youth Advocate • Community Mobilisation

My youth-development work focuses on helping young people organise themselves, discover opportunities and participate meaningfully in their communities. I have worked with youth development organisations on various projects, supporting young people and community members to establish and organise their own groups, including youth clubs, women's clubs and cooperatives.

I also assist groups with organisational processes such as registration, documentation, structuring their activities and identifying opportunities through which they can access support and community development resources, including CDF-related funding opportunities.

Beyond organisational support, my advocacy work encourages young people to develop positive livelihoods, use their talents constructively, stay engaged in community activities and explore alternatives to drug and substance abuse.

Youth & Community Engagement
05
JOURNALISM

Journalism & Media Practice

Reporter • Media Practitioner • Storyteller

My journalism experience includes interviews, community stories, public-interest reporting, field coverage and digital storytelling. I am particularly interested in stories that give communities an opportunity to be heard, understood and represented.

Reporting & Storytelling
06
CREATIVE

Film & Creative Production

Writer • Director • Creative Producer

My creative work includes screenwriting, film directing, storytelling and the development of productions that explore social issues and real-life experiences. Through filmmaking, I work with young people and emerging creatives, providing opportunities for them to participate in acting, production and other creative activities.

My productions have explored themes including relationships, marriage, faith, family, social challenges and gender-based violence. I view film not only as entertainment, but also as a powerful platform for education, awareness and social communication.

Film & Creative Production

Deputy Director • Editor • Media Coordinator

Beyond field reporting and production, I have taken on leadership and editorial responsibilities within media projects. My work includes coordinating media activities, supporting reporters and contributors, reviewing and developing stories, organising coverage and helping ensure that information reaches audiences clearly and responsibly.

I also participate in planning media coverage of community events, public activities and important local developments.

Media Leadership & Coordination
08
COMMUNITY

Community Development Support

Community Mobilisation • Organisation Support

I support individuals and community groups who want to organise themselves around common social and economic interests. This includes assisting emerging youth clubs, women's groups, cooperatives and other community initiatives with organisation, documentation and development planning.

Where appropriate, I also help groups understand available development opportunities and prepare themselves to pursue programmes and funding opportunities that may benefit their communities.

PROFESSIONAL APPROACH

Experience is more than a job title.

My professional experience has developed across several interconnected fields. Education has strengthened my ability to teach and develop people, while journalism and media have strengthened my ability to communicate, investigate and tell stories.

Youth advocacy and community development have given me opportunities to work directly with people, understand community needs and help turn ideas into practical initiatives.

Creative production has brought these experiences together by giving me another platform through which I can communicate ideas, address social issues and create opportunities for young people.
